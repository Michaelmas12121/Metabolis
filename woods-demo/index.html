The Woods story

Independent by nature.
Personal by tradition.

Woods occupies the ground floor of five Georgian townhouses in a quiet cul-de-sac opposite the Assembly Rooms. Since 1979, the Price family has built a reputation around attentive service, generous hospitality and food with a distinctly British–French point of view.

This concept preserves the restaurant’s existing story while making it easier to understand on mobile, discover menus, and book.
